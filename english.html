Use patient profiles for an individual or group portrait, or begin with a disease overview when readers need clinical and social context first.

About This Initiative

Turning patient experience into deeper understanding.

This portal brings together experiences shared through patient interviews and research, organized to deepen understanding of people living with illness. It highlights aspects that medical information alone may not capture: uncertainty before diagnosis, treatment alongside daily life, navigating information, and the people and activities that provide support.

Each account reflects an individual experience and does not represent every person with the condition. We place dignity and privacy at the center of publication.

We do more than collect voices. We understand their context and connect them to the next insight.
